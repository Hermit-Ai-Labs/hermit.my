About Hermit

Powered by NEAR
Hermit is your own AI, living on your Mac. Download the model once and it answers anywhere, even offline. Your chats, memory, files and wallet keys stay on your Mac. No account, no ads, no tracking. Private by design Hermit 1.3, the model we trained ourselves on 30 tools, runs entirely on your Mac. Turn off Wi-Fi and keep chatting. Nothing you say is uploaded. There's no Hermit server and no sign-up: you just tell Hermit your name. Optional Private Cloud for bigger questions: a larger open model on NEAR AI Cloud, inside a hardware-sealed enclave (Intel TDX and NVIDIA confidential GPUs). Hermit verifies the enclave on your Mac before sending anything, including that it runs an OS image NEAR publishes, and shows you every check. An assistant that knows you Memory: Hermit keeps the notes you choose and uses them in every chat. Projects keep chats and goals together. Automations: describe a routine in plain words and it runs on schedule while Hermit is open. Works with your calendar and files, with your permission, on your Mac. Money, on your terms A self-custody wallet: keys are created on your Mac, protected by the Secure Enclave, and never leave it. Hermit can't see or recover them. Ask in plain words: "how much NEAR would I get for 20 USDC on Base?" or "swap 20 USDC on Base to NEAR". Hermit gets a real quote through NEAR Intents and opens a clear review. Nothing moves until you approve with Touch ID. Stake NEAR from Earn: pick a validator, approve with Touch ID, unstake any time. Spending limits you set, checked on your Mac, including a cap on anything the AI prepares. Live crypto prices, markets and yields, with no account needed to browse. Hermit is an interface to public networks. We never hold your keys or funds, and nothing is bought, sold or held by us. Prices are informational, not financial advice.

Install in three steps

  1. Open the downloaded Hermit-2.0.dmg.
  2. Drag Hermit onto the Applications folder.
  3. Open Hermit. On first launch it downloads its model (about 2.8 GB, Wi-Fi is best). Then it works offline.

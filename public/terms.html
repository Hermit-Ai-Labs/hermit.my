08NEAR features

  • Swaps and bridges. NEAR Intents and Relay quotes are filled by third parties. Hermit checks a quote's signature and shows it to you, but delivery depends on those services. If a swap fails, refunds go to the refund address in the quote, under those services' rules.
  • Staking. Staked NEAR is delegated to a validator's staking pool you choose. Rewards aren't guaranteed and can change. Unstaked NEAR is locked for about four epochs (roughly 28 hours) before you can withdraw it. Validators can perform badly or go offline.
  • Hermit Black Box. Hermit Black Box is a smart contract on NEAR mainnet (hermit-blackbox.near). It is optional and off until you turn it on. When it's on, each AI move you approve leaves a fingerprint on the blockchain: a salted hash and a short kind, never your chat, amounts or addresses. You keep the text behind each fingerprint on your device. Records on the blockchain can't be edited or removed.
  • Hermit AI insurance. Hermit AI insurance is a smart contract on NEAR mainnet (hermit-insurance.near). It is optional, and it unlocks once the Black Box is on, because a claim needs its records. 1 USDC buys 30 days of cover, and all of it goes into a public pool. If the AI prepared something that cost you money, you can file a claim with its Black Box record. An AI reviewer writes a verdict, and Hermit decides each claim at its discretion. An approved claim is paid in USDC from the pool, as far as the pool allows. Cover is not a guarantee of payment. The evidence text you file with a claim is public on the blockchain. Smart contracts can have bugs, and you use them at your own risk.
  • Hermit Guard. Hermit Guard is a smart contract on NEAR mainnet (hermit-guard.near). Funds you put in a Guard vault are held by the contract under the rules you set, not by us. You can withdraw them with your own key. Anyone can trigger a standing order once it's due, and it pays only what your rules allow. Smart contracts can have bugs, and blockchain upgrades or network problems can affect them. The contract's code is public in our repository, and you use it at your own risk.

09Trading, perps and yield

  • Perpetual futures and leverage carry high risk. A position can be liquidated, and you can lose everything you put in, quickly.
  • Orders, stops and take profits are placed on the venue you choose, such as Hyperliquid. They can fill at a different price, partly fill or not fill at all. Venues set their own minimums, margin rules and fees.
  • Yields, lending and liquidity products, such as Aave or pools shown in Earn, can lose value through smart-contract failures, depegs, bad debt or market moves. Rates shown are past or current rates, not promises.
  • Tokens, especially new or small ones, can be scams, lose all their value, or be impossible to sell. Hermit showing a token doesn't mean we've checked or endorse it.

Questions, answered.

What is Hermit?

A loyal AI agent that runs on your computer. It chats like any assistant, and it can earn, trade and pay for you, but only after you approve.

Is it really local?

Yes. The model, your chat history and your private keys live on your machine. Nothing you type is sent to us.

Can it spend my money on its own?

No. The assistant never moves money without your approval: every payment shows a plan and a hazard scan first, then waits for you. You set the budget and the daily limit. If you want something to pay without asking each time, Budgets lets you set aside a small amount on NEAR with rules, for standing orders and for agents you give a key to.

What does "uncensored" mean here?

Hermit answers adult questions plainly, without lectures or refusals for the sake of it. It still won't help anyone hurt people.

Can I stake NEAR?

Yes. Open Earn, pick a validator and approve with Face ID or Touch ID. You can unstake any time. You can also ask Hermit: "stake 5 NEAR".

Where does NEAR fit in?

Hermit is built on NEAR for six things: NEAR Intents for swaps and bridges across chains, NEAR AI Cloud for optional private answers from a bigger model, a NEAR account in every wallet (you can stake from it), the Black box, hermit-blackbox.near, which publishes our models' hashes and fingerprints every AI money move, AI insurance, hermit-insurance.near, which pays you back from a public pool when the AI's mistake costs you money, and Budgets, our NEAR contract hermit-guard.near, which holds spending rules for standing orders and agents. Hermit is its own app, built on NEAR.

Is it on my phone?

Yes. The iPhone app is in beta on TestFlight: tap Download and pick iPhone.
